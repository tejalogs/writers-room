# Lite cheat sheet (for small models)

Everything a small model needs to write well, on one page. The full craft files say the same things at length.

## Truth
- Never invent a story, quote, number, result or person. Missing → `[NEEDS STORY: …]` or `[NEEDS SOURCE: …]`.
- Quote real people word for word.

## The idea
- One idea per piece. Write it in 20 words or fewer before drafting: the **spine**.
- Name the belief the reader holds and the truth that replaces it.

## The first line
- Start with a real moment, a surprising fact, a flat contradiction or a question about the reader.
- Never start with a greeting, a definition, "In this article" or "Have you ever".
- The first line must make the reader need the second.

## Moving through the piece
- Join ideas with **but** or **so**, never **and then**.
- Ask a question early; answer it late.
- End each section on something unresolved, not a summary.

## Sentences
- Concrete beats vague: a number, a name, a time, an object, someone's exact words.
- Mix short and long sentences. Put the strongest word last.
- Active voice. Plain words. No "very", "really", "just".

## The end
- Return to the opening image, now seen differently.
- Give one thing to do today.

## The brand
- The reader is the hero. Mention the brand late, once or twice, as the guide with a method.
