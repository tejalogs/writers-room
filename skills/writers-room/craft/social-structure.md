# Social Structure — posts, threads, captions and descriptions

Social writing is the hook stack compressed. The feed gives you one line, sometimes two, before the "see more" cut. Everything above the cut decides whether anything below it exists.

## Shared rules

1. **The first line must work alone.** On LinkedIn and Instagram only ~1–2 lines show before "see more". Write it last, after you know the payoff.
2. **One idea, one story.** A post is a single beat: moment → turn → point.
3. **White space is pacing.** Short paragraphs; one-line paragraphs for the turn.
4. **End on a point or a real question**, not "Thoughts?" and not a hashtag wall. 0–3 relevant hashtags at most, at the end.
5. **Native, not repurposed-looking.** Never paste a blog intro. Rewrite for the platform's rhythm.
6. Every rule in `principles.md` still applies: true, specific, the reader is the hero.

## By platform

| Platform | Shape | Length | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn post** | Hook line → short story or observation → the turn → the point → question or takeaway | 120–250 words | Personal "I saw / I was wrong" framing performs; avoid "I'm humbled to announce" |
| **X / Threads post** | One sharp claim or observation | ≤ 280 characters | The hook *is* the post |
| **X thread** | Hook post (promise + count: "5 things...") → one idea per post → closing post that pays off the hook | 5–8 posts | Each post must stand alone if quoted |
| **Instagram caption** | Hook line (expands the image) → short story → point → soft CTA | 80–200 words | Hook must not repeat what the image already says |
| **YouTube / podcast description** | Line 1: the episode's promise in searchable words → 2–3 sentence summary → chapters → links | 100–250 words | First ~150 characters show in search; put the keyword there |
| **Short-video script (Reels/Shorts/TikTok)** | Hook (0–2 s, said *and* on screen) → setup → turn → payoff → loop back to the start | 60–150 spoken words (20–60 s) | Write it as an A/V table with shots and on-screen text: see `video-structure.md` |

## The short pipeline for social

Social pieces run a shortened pipeline: **01 brief → 02 mine → 03 angle (5 hooks, not 10) → 05 draft (2–3 variants) → 06 critique (Truth, Hook Doctor, Specificity, Sceptical Reader only) → 07 polish.** Skip the outline and the package stage. Lint with `--mode podcast` for spoken scripts; for written posts, run the linter's blog checks mentally (it's tuned for long-form, so its length and heading checks don't apply).

## Example (illustrative; details invented for teaching)

> She replied to a rejection email.
>
> Not angrily. Two sentences: "I understand. Could you tell me one thing I could have done better?"
>
> It was her fortieth rejection. She told me it felt like begging.
>
> The recruiter answered the same day, with two specific points about her interview.
>
> Forty companies said no. One told her why. The only difference was that she asked.
>
> When did you last ask?

Why it works: line one is a complete, surprising event; the turn ("The only difference was that she asked") gets its own line; it ends on a question aimed at the reader, not "Thoughts?".
