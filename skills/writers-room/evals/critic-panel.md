# The Critic Panel

Seven critics. Each reads the draft alone, cares only about its own job, and scores 1–5:
**5** = would hold up next to the best in the world · **4** = ship · **3** = fixable with edits · **2** = structural problem · **1** = start again.

## Rules for every critic

Open-ended "critique and improve this" loops show no reliable gain, and a model reviewing its own work tends to like it. So each critic works like a checklist, not a vibe:

1. **Every finding quotes the exact sentence** (or names the beat) and names **which question below it fails**. No quote, no finding.
2. **No general notes** like "improve the flow" or "make it more engaging". If you can't point to a sentence, it isn't a finding.
3. **Propose a specific fix** for each finding, but don't rewrite the draft. Stage 7 does the rewriting.
4. **Run each critic in a separate pass** (a separate call for small or mid-size models), and preferably on a different model from the one that drafted.
5. **Who may score:** a large model or a human. A small model may run the Truth Editor's mechanical checks (does each quote appear verbatim in the story bank; does each number have a source) but should leave the 1–5 scores to a larger model.

---

## 1. The Truth Editor (runs first; any failure blocks shipping)
- Is every story, quote and number traceable to the story bank or a cited source?
- Are quotes verbatim? Are outcomes stated only if the source states them?
- Any claim that overreaches the evidence ("always", "guaranteed", "everyone")?
- Any compliance rule from `BRAND.md` broken?
- Any vague attribution ("experts say", "studies show") with no named source?
- Any frequency claim ("most people", "almost nobody", "everyone") without evidence? Any invented thought or feeling attributed to a real person? Any first-person experience not in the story bank or author bio? (Check the repurposed social cuts in the package too.)
- **Protected content (after polish):** does every story-bank quote, every `[NEEDS ...]` marker and every link from the draft survive into `final.md` unchanged, unless deliberately removed with a note?
Score 5 only if there is nothing to check. Any invention = 1.

## 2. The Hook Doctor
- Does line 1 create a specific gap? Does line 2 deepen it?
- Is the hook's promise paid off explicitly, and where?
- Competitor swap test on the first 100 words.
- Any throat-clearing before the hook?

## 3. The Structure Editor
- Spine: can you state the one idea in 20 words from the draft alone?
- Bridges: read section openings in order: any "and then"?
- Loops: all opened loops closed? Count never drops to zero before the end?
- Does each section end on a forward pull, not a summary?
- Does the close call back to the opening?

## 4. The Specificity Hunter
- Mark every sentence that could appear in any article on this topic. More than 20% = score ≤ 3.
- At least one concrete detail per ~100 words?
- Are examples real and particular, or hypothetical ("imagine...")?

## 5. The Ear (podcast) / The Scanner (blog)
- **Ear:** read aloud. Any stumble, breath-break mid-sentence, ambiguous pronoun, number pile-up? Re-hook spacing ≤ ~90 s? Is the key idea said twice?
- **Scanner:** read only title, dek, H2s, bold sentences and takeaways. Do they tell the whole story and make you want the body?

## 6. The Sceptical Reader (the target audience persona from the brief)
- Would I believe this? Where do I roll my eyes?
- Does it understand my situation better than I could describe it?
- Do I know what to do on Monday?
- Is the brand mention earned, or does it feel like an ad?
- Where the brand appears, does it sell an outcome I can picture, or list features? Does it declare ("excellent guidance") or show?

## 7. The format specialist (pick the one that matches the format)
- **Search Strategist (blogs, organic landing pages):** intent match, answer block, H2s as real questions, self-contained sections, information gain present, FAQ quality, internal links, attributable claims.
- **Producer (podcasts):** runtime vs target, clip-worthy moments (at least 3), cold open strong enough to work as a trailer, two-host lines not filler.
- **Director (video):** read only the Visual column. Does it tell the story with the sound off? Is there a hook shot in the first 2–3 seconds? Does every beat have a shot that shows rather than illustrates the voice-over? Is the storytelling device visible? Do the on-screen text cards add to the VO instead of repeating it?
- **Media Buyer (ads, ad landing pages):** does line 1 or shot 1 stop the right person on its own? Does the opening match the audience's awareness level? Does the ad's promise match the landing page headline? Is there one clear CTA (verb + outcome)? Is everything within platform limits and policy (no personal-attribute assertions, no unsupported health or finance claims, no fake urgency)?
- **Conversion Editor (landing pages):** five-second test on the hero; one goal and one primary CTA; features shown as outcomes; proof real and next to its claim; the objections people actually raise answered.
